I’m building my portfolio through practical business analysis case studies. My work focuses on defining business problems, understanding who is affected, and deciding how to measure improvement.

## Case Studies

### [Clinic Booking Center](./clinic-booking-center.md)

*Training simulation*

The booking center received complaints about overlapping appointments across four clinics. I defined the problem, identified the people affected, and drafted interview questions to understand how bookings are handled. I also defined what the analysis would cover and proposed a four-week baseline to measure how often conflicts occur.

**Skills demonstrated** · Problem definition, stakeholder analysis, interview planning, scope definition, and KPI design.
