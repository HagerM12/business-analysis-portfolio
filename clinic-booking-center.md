# Clinic Booking Center | Business Analysis Case Study

*Training simulation · W01-A*

## Problem Statement

Two staff members manage appointments for four clinics using a paper logbook and a separate schedule.  
The booking center has received complaints about overlapping appointments. It has yet to measure how often they occur or establish what causes them.

## Affected Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Potential impact |
|---|---|
| Booking staff | May need to check both records, contact patients, and rearrange appointments. |
| Patients with conflicting appointments | May have to wait longer or reschedule their visits. |
| Doctors at the affected clinics | May face disruptions to their appointment schedules. |
| Booking center manager | May need to follow up on complaints and allocate staff time to resolve conflicts. |

## Interview Questions

1. Can you walk me through the last appointment you booked, from how the request came in to how you confirmed it?
2. How do you share responsibility for booking the four clinics, and where does each of you record appointments?
3. How do you check that a slot is free before confirming it? What happens if two requests for the same slot come in close together?
4. Can you tell me about the last time two appointments conflicted? How did you find out, and what happened to the patients’ appointments?
5. For appointments scheduled last week, how many advance bookings were confirmed, how many conflicts can we verify, and how many bookings were affected? Which records can we check?

## Measurable Objective

Over four weeks, track the weekly number of verified booking conflicts and the percentage of confirmed advance bookings they affect across the four clinics. Use those figures as the baseline and agree on a reduction target with the booking center manager. After the booking process changes, track both measures for another four weeks and compare the results.

**Weekly conflict rate** = (Confirmed advance bookings affected by a verified conflict ÷ All confirmed advance bookings) × 100.

Count bookings in the week their appointments were scheduled to take place. If two patients are booked into a slot that can accommodate only one, count it as one conflict affecting two bookings.

## In Scope

1. How staff record advance bookings, check availability, and confirm appointments for the four clinics.
2. How staff identify and resolve conflicts between confirmed advance bookings, and how often those conflicts occur.

## Out of Scope

1. Walk-in visits without a prior appointment.
2. Clinical care and waiting times unrelated to booking conflicts.
